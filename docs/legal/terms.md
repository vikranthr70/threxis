### Purpose and scope

THREXIS is a research prototype demonstrating threat-informed forensic readiness and incident investigation using artificial records. It is provided for local development and academic demonstrations.

### Interpretation of outputs

Readiness is a documented weighted preparedness indicator. Detection alerts, ATT&CK candidates, correlations, and ranking weights support investigation and do not establish that a compromise occurred. Synthetic demonstrations do not establish real-world detection effectiveness.

SHA-256 verification compares bytes against an expected digest. It does not establish evidence provenance, legal admissibility, or a complete chain of custody. Local manifests and audit files can be changed.

### Permitted data

Use synthetic records and non-sensitive research results only. Do not submit personal information, live credentials, malware, confidential incident evidence, or unauthorized telemetry. The application does not provide attack execution or endpoint collection capabilities.
